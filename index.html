THE LAST SORRY

Sorry, jaanu.

Agar ek apology se sab kuch instantly perfect ho jata, toh shayad sorry ka matlab hi chhota hota. Main tumse perfection nahi maang raha. Main bas apni galti ko acknowledge kar raha hoon, tumhari feelings ko respect kar raha hoon, aur apne actions ko better karne ki responsibility accept kar raha hoon.

Tumhe abhi jo feel karna hai, woh feel kar sakti ho. Tumhe time chahiye toh time. Tumhe baat karni hai toh baat. Tumhe bas ye page close karna hai toh woh bhi okay. Ye experience tumhe pressure dene ke liye nahi tha — bas ek honest “I'm sorry” tum tak pahunchane ke liye tha.

And one last line…

I am genuinely sorry. ♥
